    Be a Linux Foundation member, including through an LF umbrella foundation, or qualify for LF Associate Membership.

A few things to know

Can I participate without an OSPO or a member organization?

Yes. Individuals are welcome regardless of employment, affiliation, or experience. Come to learn, share, or contribute at your own pace.

Can I contribute on behalf of my organization?

Yes. You can share organizational experience, contribute resources, and collaborate in working groups. Formal General Membership is available to eligible organizations that want to support TODO in that capacity.

Where should I start?

Start by joining TODO Slack, where our community connects and has virtual conversations. Then explore a working group or a community gathering. All participation follows the TODO Code of Conduct.
