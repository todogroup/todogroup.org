TODO Group official

OSPOlogy events

Organized or co-organized by TODO Group with adjacent and local communities, in two formats:

At organizations’ venues and offices

Standalone OSPOlogy events with workshops, panels, and roundtable discussions, hosted at organizations’ venues or offices.

Co-located with Linux Foundation events

OSPOlogy gatherings alongside events such as KubeCon + CloudNativeCon and PyTorch Conference, connecting OSPO practitioners with the wider open source ecosystem.

Community-led

Community-led OSPOlogy meetups

Gatherings led by local organizers and TODO ambassadors, shaped around the interests, languages, and needs of their communities.

  • In-person, hybrid, and online meetups
  • Local conversations and practical experience sharing
  • Connections with nearby practitioners and communities

This is OSPOlogy

TODO Group’s event brand for learning and collaborating across the open source ecosystem.

OSPOlogy brings OSPO practitioners together with adjacent and local communities to exchange experience, work through shared challenges, and build connections that continue beyond the event.

Connecting adjacent communities

  • OpenChain
  • CNCF
  • OpenSSF
  • CHAOSS
  • CURIOSS
  • InnerSource Commons

Rooted in local communities

Collaborations also connect local organizers and regional communities, including the Linux Foundation APAC OSPO community in China.
