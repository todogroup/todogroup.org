We're excited to welcome [Swastik Baranwal](https://github.com/Delta456) as the newest [TODO Group Ambassador](https://todogroup.org/community/ambassadors/) and the first TODO Ambassador based in India. Swastik is an active contributor and leader in the TODO community, serving as a co-chair of the OSS in Business Working Group and contributing to initiatives including OSPOlogy, the TODO website, the OSPO Book, and the TODO newsletter. His ongoing community support helps keep TODO Group initiatives active, relevant, and up to date.

## Bio and Contributions to TODO Project

Swastik's contributions to the TODO ecosystem include:

- Co-chairing the OSS in Business Working Group and helping advance its work on the business value of open source
- Contributing to TODO resources and community initiatives, including the website, newsletter, OSPO Book, and OSPOlogy
- Beginning his TODO journey by participating in the [TODO BoF at KubeCon + CloudNativeCon India 2024](https://github.com/todogroup/ospology/tree/main/BoF/India2024_Report)
- Serving as one of the beta testers for the [Certified Open Source Developer for Enterprise (CODE)](https://training.linuxfoundation.org/certification/certified-open-source-developer-for-enterprise-code/) exam
- Serving on the OSPO track program committee for [Open Source Summit Japan 2025](https://www.credly.com/badges/96656df7-6491-442c-a1ba-19d91db0642b/public_url)

We look forward to seeing how Swastik will continue supporting the TODO community and helping keep its initiatives active, useful, and up to date as a TODO Ambassador.
